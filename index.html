How this works. Each past question is grouped with earlier questions on the same idea. For every resulting concept, the model looks at how often it appears, how recently, and how regularly it cycles back — then a logistic regression (trained on your own earlier papers, tested only against papers it never saw) turns that into a probability for the upcoming paper. The chapter-level weightage uses whichever of a few simple trend models has predicted your actual papers best historically.

This is a revision-priority tool built from public patterns in your own papers — not a leaked paper and not a guarantee. Treat a high rank as "study this first," not "this exact question will appear."
